Battle Arena LIVE - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Information We May Process

Battle Arena LIVE may process information associated with livestream interactions, such as public usernames, event types, gift names, interaction values, and timestamps, when such information is made available through an authorized integration.

2. How Information Is Used

Information received from livestream interactions may be used to display viewer contributions, update game events, maintain temporary leaderboards, and provide interactive entertainment during livestreams.

3. Data Minimization

The application is designed to use only information necessary for the interactive game experience. It does not intentionally request passwords, payment information, or private messages.

4. Third-Party Services

Battle Arena LIVE may interact with third-party platforms. Those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Retention

Viewer interaction data may be processed temporarily for the duration of a game session. Data retention may vary depending on the integration and configuration used by the application.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions regarding Battle Arena LIVE, please contact the application owner through the contact method associated with the project.
